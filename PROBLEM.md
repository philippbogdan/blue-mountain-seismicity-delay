# The problem

A geothermal reservoir run as energy storage is pressurised and drawn down daily, and each pressurisation brings small induced earthquakes. In the one fibre-monitored field test of such cycling, at Blue Mountain, Nevada, in 2023, seismicity rose hours after reservoir pressure, and operators are advised to steer towards longer delays. That works only if the delay answers to operation. If it is the time pore pressure takes to diffuse to the faults, the site fixes it. If it is the faults' own delay under a changing load, the pressurisation rate sets it, and the ramp schedule becomes a lever on seismicity.

## To find

What sets the timing and shape of the seismicity response to each pressure cycle at Blue Mountain, how it depends on distance and pressurisation rate, what it would be under slower ramps, and what ramp schedule and monitoring at a future cycled reservoir would settle what these data cannot.

## Given

- The data, in data/osf-d65ba/ (doi:10.17605/OSF.IO/D65BA, CC BY 4.0): a catalogue of 13,351 events from one fibre in monitoring well 73-22, the cycling-period events' vertical distances from the injector, the pressure gauge in 73-22, and injection and production rates. Five cycles ran from 30 April to 5 May 2023.
- The catalogue's time zone is not stated, while the pressure series is labelled PST. An offset of seven or eight hours is the size of the delay itself.
- The paper, doi:10.1029/2025JB031634 (text in data/paper/), is the floor. It describes the delay only qualitatively and fits one diffusion front, D ≈ 0.43 m²/s.
- Fault-zone hydraulic diffusivity at Blue Mountain from well tidal responses: 0.08 to 0.33 m²/s (doi:10.1029/2021GL093986).

## Why it is not routine

Ramp rate was never varied on purpose: injection was held constant, and the pressure path followed production curtailment over five cycles. One fibre locates events only by depth and distance from the well, and pressure is known only at the monitoring well, some 200 m from the injector. Diffusion and a rate-dependent fault response both make delays that change with distance and loading rate.
